<objective>
Close the single blocking gap in 01-VERIFICATION.md: success criterion 4 / OLLAMA-05. The AI
service health surface never reports Ollama Cloud at all, because `HealthServiceProvider::boot()`
freezes the check list from the fresh-install config seed before `ChatServiceProvider::boot()`
overlays the live, settings-backed catalog onto `config('chat.models')`.

Purpose: `ChatProviderCheck::forConfiguredProviders()` must observe the catalog as it stands after
every provider has booted, not mid-boot. Ollama Cloud is the first provider whose servability
depends entirely on that overlay, which is precisely what D-04 designed: its seed rows in
`packages/Chat/config/chat.php` carry `capabilities: null` because pricing and capabilities cannot
be filled honestly at seed time and are measured later by `ModelProbe`. `CatalogEntry::isServable()`
requires a non-null measurement, so both D-06 rows (`gpt-oss:20b`, `gpt-oss:120b`) are filtered out
of `reachableModels()` and zero `ollama_cloud` checks register. Anthropic and OpenAI are unaffected
only because their seed rows ship pre-filled capabilities.

Output: a two-line deferral in `app/Providers/HealthServiceProvider.php`, plus a regression test in
`tests/Feature/HealthChecks/HealthServiceProviderTest.php` that asserts through a real, full
application bootstrap (not a hand-constructed provider instance) that a provider servable only via
the live settings overlay does register a check.

**Decisions honored.** This plan touches only the consequences of D-04 (null pricing and null seed
capabilities for Ollama Cloud rows) and D-06 (the two `gpt-oss` rows that expose the defect). It
re-opens nothing. D-01 (the `parallel_tool_calls` write-guard investigation, resolved to the
documented `prompt` fallback), D-02 (Pro tier concurrency assumption), D-03 (`auto: false` for every
Ollama Cloud row) and D-05 (per-model `credit_multiplier` confirmed with the user) were delivered
and verified by plans 01-01, 01-02 and 01-03 and are out of scope here.

**Explicitly out of scope.** Success criterion 5 / OLLAMA-06 carries a user resolution dated
2026-09-03 in 01-VERIFICATION.md: it does not block phase sign-off and is tracked separately at
`.planning/todos/pending/2026-09-03-chat-post-approval-defects-no-success-message-broken-turn-co.md`.
No task here addresses it. No HTTP health dashboard route is added: `grep -rn health routes/` returns
nothing today, the CLI is the health surface this codebase actually has, and adding a route is not
in the VERIFICATION.md `missing` list.
